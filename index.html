VERITY — Market Prediction Platform | Practice Mode for Risk-Free Crypto Price Prediction (BTC & ETH)

VERITY is a skill-based market prediction platform where users predict crypto price movements (BTC and ETH) through a unique dual-mode experience. Practice Mode gives you 1,000 free points to learn market analysis without financial risk. Mainnet Mode offers real stakes for experienced predictors.

Platform features include: 48 daily prediction pools with 30-minute rounds, peer-to-peer competition with loser-funded rewards, accuracy-based reputation badges from Beginner to Legend, real-time price feeds from CoinCap API, and comprehensive performance analytics.

Badge progression system: Beginner (first prediction, 100 pts cap), Learner (10 predictions, 250 pts), Rooky (25 predictions, 500 pts), Analyst (50 predictions + 55% WR, 1,000 pts), Trader (100 predictions + 60% WR, 2,500 pts), Expert (250 predictions + 65% WR, 5,000 pts), Strategist (500 predictions + 70% WR, 10,000 pts), Master (1,000 predictions + 75% WR, 25,000 pts), Legend (2,500+ predictions + 80% WR, 100,000 pts).

Current platform metrics: 200+ active Practice Mode users, 48 daily pools automated, 100% platform uptime, average stake per pool 100-600 points, 65% of users make at least 5 predictions, top 10% average 48 predictions.

Key features: Wallet-based identity (EVM compatible), free Practice Mode with 1,000 starting points, 48 daily BTC and ETH pools, public leaderboards (accuracy & volume), user profiles with performance history, dual-mode architecture, loser-funded reward system (platform never becomes counterparty).
